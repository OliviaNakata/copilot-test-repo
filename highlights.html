- Highlights -

2025-2026

  • Powderpuff
    Powderpuff is the big traditional event in Brookline High School, which typically holds in November at Parsons Field, Brookline, MA. Last year, we performed “Strategy” by Twice, “Mantra” by Jennie, and “Bicycle Ride” by VYBZ KARTEL during the half- time show of Powderpuff.
  • Revision
    Revision is a annual talent show held at the audotorium in Tappan building. Last year, we performed “Chk Chk Boom” by Stray Kids, and “Supernatural” by NewJeans during Revision
  • Lunar New Year
    Every year before the LNY holiday, Asian Pacific American Club(APAC) holds the special showcase at the Brookline High School's audotorium. Last year, we performed “10 minutes” by Lee Hyolee, “Sugarcoat” by Natty during Lunar New Year show.
  • Progression
    Progression is a biggest dance performance showcase at Brokkline High School featuring our school's dancers, faculty, and student-led groups. Last year, we performed “Exes” by Tate McRae, “Rude Boy” by Rhiana during Progression.
  • K-night at BHS
    K-night is a showcase that combines our school's, HHS', LHS' and so many other school's k-pop dance clubs! Last year, we performed “Cherish” by ILLIT, “Tell Me” by NewJeans, “Fashion” by Cortis, “Rumor” by IZ*ONE, “Outside” by Enhypen, “Dirty Work” by Aespa, “Gabriela” by Katseye and so much more!
  • K-Club Showcase at HHS
    We are also perfoming at Hopkinton High School! Last year, we performed “Hands Up” by MEOVV, “This is For” by Twice, and “Hype Boy” by NewJeans.
  • Lex K-night at LHS
    We are also performing at Lexington High School! Last year, We performed “Go” by Cortis, and “Sign” by Izna.
