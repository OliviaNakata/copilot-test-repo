Interested to involve in our community? Start from here -> We have a HUGE announcement to all of BHS students! The Powderpuff audition application is now open, and we want to invite all of the BHS students who are passionate about dance or kpop to this project!! This year, we will perform "Leak it" by FLO, "Greedy" and "Dear God" by Tate McRae. Indeed, this is not a kpop, but the chreography is created from prominent Korean professional dancers: "Dear God" is chreographied by ROSHE (from @justjerkacademy), and "Greedy" is chreographied by Harimu (from @1milliondance). Last but not least, "Leak it" is chreographied by Nakyung (also from @1milliondance), which is going to be our biggest finale at powderpuff!!!

Again, Powderpuff is one of the biggest traditional event at BHS! You have to be comfortable enough to perform in front of a whole school... Last year, a total of 28 dancers participated at Powderpuff!! This the time to discover your new talent:D

How to Apply

apply to our audition ASAP! The audition will be held ONLY IN PERSON, Sep 24th at DANCE STUDIO 1, Tappan Gym.

Greedy (5 people in total)
      1. Take the audition of "Dear God" + "Leak It" in person on September 24 (Thursday).
      2. The 5 EXCEPTIONAL dancers from "Dear God" will be chosen for "Greedy." in person on September 24 (Thursday).

**People in Greedy MUST perform "Dear God" and "Leak It"**

Dear God (TBD)

**People in "Dear God" MUST perform "Leak It" as well.**

Leak It (TBD)
