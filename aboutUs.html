- About Us -

Established in 2012, Popcorn club is a student-led club which aims to spread k-pop culture and engage students who are passionate about k-pop. The goal of this club is to encourage students building a community, enhance cooperation and communication skills, gain the leadership experience by teaching other members. The club runs throughout a year, a weekly meeting/pracitce holds on every Monday and Thursday. The current club does not provide any prerequisites to join the club, and all students are welcome!
